# Project conventions

- Product name: **Vector ERP** in English, **Вектор ERP** in Ukrainian; Docker project and image: `vector-erp`. The internal Frappe package remains `warehouse_core`. The AI assistant is **Vectoria ✦ AI-assistant** in English and **Векторія ✦ ШІ-помічник** in Ukrainian.

- Use natural English for English app names, modules, role profiles, package names, and routes (for example, `Warehouse Core`, `warehouse_core`, and `warehouse-core`). Do not transliterate Ukrainian words into English identifiers.
- Use Ukrainian normally in Ukrainian interface labels and client documentation.
- Keep the Telegram bot's `/help` current in the same change whenever user-facing menus, commands, workflows, AI capabilities, access, limits, or availability change. Update the shared help text in `services/telegram_bot/content.json`, preserve owner-only AI guidance, verify help against the actual behavior, and update the relevant Telegram documentation before deployment.
- Use the app's styled tooltip component whenever a tooltip is needed; do not use browser-default `title` tooltips. The top-panel Search and Notifications buttons should have no tooltip; retain their accessible labels.
- Keep the proper name MoySklad when referring to the source product and its external IDs. Legacy identifiers may remain only where needed for migration or existing persistent infrastructure.
- Preserve existing business records and role assignments when renaming configuration. Verify the installed application and transaction checks after a deployment change.
- Treat AI usage alerts as a required part of any Vector ERP deployment: deploy the spending guard and persistent Telegram delivery queue with the ERP, verify 75/95% thresholds and the independent 95% cutoff, and confirm a deployment-specific test message reaches the owner's private Telegram chat. The owner removed the 50% and 85% alerts on 2026-11-04. Do not mark deployment complete with alerts missing or dependent on the owner's computer. Configure the local usage budget and verify pricing before enabling AI. See `docs/ai-spending-control.uk.md` for deployment acceptance; usage alerts are Telegram-only.
- On the MTech website, do not use decorative sequential numbering such as `01`, `02`, `03` on articles, cards, sections, service steps, or other interface labels. Use descriptive labels without numeric prefixes. Apply this rule to templates, generated pages, and archived concepts as well. Preserve meaningful numbers such as dates, prices, quantities, technical specifications, and product identifiers.
- Automatic AI Telegram notifications are limited to 75% and 95% usage. Do not send stale-balance, credit-expiry, pricing-verification, or uncertain-request notices; retain pricing verification and uncertain-request holds. Preserve delivered history and suppress retired notices still queued.
- The owner stopped repeated AI Telegram delivery tests on 2026-11-04. Keep `AI_TELEGRAM_TESTS_ENABLED=false`; do not send test messages or enable this switch during routine edits, local deployments, restarts, or verification. Reuse existing delivery receipts and offline tests. A new server's first delivery acceptance requires the owner's explicit request for that test; do not claim it has passed without a receipt. Preserve delivered history and suppress pending test notices.
- The owner removed API funding, balance, exclusivity and grant-expiry checks on 2026-11-05. Use the configured local budget and persistent usage ledger for the independent 95% cutoff and the 75/95% Telegram notifications. Never require confirming provider funds or extending a funding date to use AI.
